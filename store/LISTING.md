## Detailed description

Make WHO Blue Books easier to read with adjustable typography, clearer navigation, and a responsive reading layout.

Blue Books Reader is an independent extension for people who already have authorized access to the WHO Classification of Tumours website. It preserves the source text, figures, citations, links, captions, and reading order. It does not summarize, translate, rewrite, or add medical information.

READ AT YOUR OWN PACE

Adjust text size, maximum text width, and line spacing independently. Read articles using the page's main scroll, with a sticky section outline on wide screens. Return to your recent reading position when navigating back in the same tab.

FIND YOUR PLACE

Jump to existing sections and subheadings. Expand individual chapters or use a book's control to expand or collapse all its chapters. Long outlines can be opened in groups without hiding the article itself.

KEEP THE DETAILS

Read tables with their original cell relationships and footnotes intact. Browse figures with their original identifiers and captions. Turn the reading layout off from the popup whenever you prefer the original presentation.

LOCAL PROCESSING

Page content is processed in your browser. Preferences stay in Chrome. Supported page paths, scroll positions, and timestamps are stored in the website's tab session to restore your reading position; records older than four hours are not used. The extension has no analytics, ads, or external data transmission. See the privacy policy for details.

ACCESS AND COMPATIBILITY

An existing authorized account or subscription to tumourclassification.iarc.who.int is required. This extension does not unlock paid content or include any books. It supports the site's recognized chapter and article layouts; not every volume or edition has been tested, and some standalone attachment pages retain the site's native layout.

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by WHO or IARC. It is a reading aid, not a source of medical advice. Screenshots use clearly labelled original sample content, not book excerpts.
